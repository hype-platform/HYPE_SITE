This policy explains how we find violations, what penalties apply, and how to appeal. It is part of the [Terms of Service](/terms). We may depart from it whenever we judge that safety, the law or the facts of a case require it.

## 1. How we find violations

We learn of violations through user reports, our own review, rights holder notices, NCMEC, law enforcement, and other trusted organizations.

## 2. How we review

People who run hypefindr review reports, and may use automated tools to help. We may look at the reported content, the account's history, linked accounts, and reliable information from outside hypefindr. We aim to review reports within 24 hours, and faster for threats, child safety and intimate images.

## 3. Severity tiers

## 4. Strikes

Strikes for Tier 2 violations expire after 180 days. Strikes for Tier 3 and integrity violations expire after 90 days. Active strikes add up:

1. **First strike:** content removed and a warning.
2. **Second strike:** feature limits for up to 7 days, such as no streaming, hosting or messaging.
3. **Third strike:** account suspended for 7 days.
4. **Fourth strike:** account suspended for 30 days.
5. **Fifth strike:** permanent ban.

We may skip steps, apply a penalty more severe than this table, or ban on a first violation when the harm or risk is serious.

## 5. Feature penalties and live intervention

We may remove specific features, including streaming, hosting events, messaging, check-ins, Crews, invites and appearance in rankings. During a live stream we may mute it, turn off chat, add a warning screen or end it.

## 6. Suspensions and bans

- **Suspension:** you cannot use your account, and your profile and content may be hidden until the suspension ends.
- **Permanent ban:** the ban applies to you as a person, not only the account. You may not create or use any other account, and we may disable any account we believe is yours. We may remove your public content.
- **Ban evasion:** any new account is removed when found, and the original ban may be extended.
- **Off-platform conduct and criminal charges:** we may suspend an account while charges or credible allegations of violence or sexual misconduct are pending, and ban it if they are substantiated.

## 7. Notice

We usually notify you in the app or by email, with the rule broken and the action taken. We may not notify you, or may delay notice, if notice could endanger someone, interfere with an investigation, help someone evade detection, or break the law.

## 8. Appeals

1. **How:** use the appeal option in the notice, or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the subject "Appeal," your username, and why you think we made a mistake.
2. **When:** within 30 days of the decision.
3. **Who reviews:** where possible, someone who did not make the original decision.
4. **Timing:** we aim to decide within 14 days.
5. **Outcome:** we uphold, reverse or change the decision. If we reverse it, we restore content where we still can and remove the strike.
6. **One appeal per decision.** Our appeal decision is final for purposes of the Services. Section 28 of the Terms governs any legal claim.

An appeal does not pause a penalty. Reports to NCMEC and law enforcement are not withdrawn because of an appeal.

## 9. Reporters

We keep the identity of people who report confidential, except where the law requires disclosure. Knowingly false reports, mass reporting and abuse of the appeal process are violations.

## 10. Records

We keep enforcement records, reports and appeals for the periods in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy), and we preserve content and data when the law requires it or law enforcement asks.

## 11. Our discretion

Enforcement decisions are made by hypefindr in its sole discretion. Not acting on one violation does not prevent us from acting on another. We have no liability for any enforcement decision, or for any decision not to enforce, to the fullest extent the law allows. We may publish aggregated reports about our enforcement.
