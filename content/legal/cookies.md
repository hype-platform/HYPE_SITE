hypefindr.net currently sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. This notice covers the website only. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) covers the app.

## 1. What the website collects

| What | Why | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Server logs kept by our hosting provider: IP address, browser and device type, page requested (including any invite code in the link), referring page, date and time | Delivering pages, security, preventing abuse | Up to 30 days, unless needed to investigate abuse |
| Emails you send to the address listed on the site | Answering you | As described in the Privacy Policy |

Fonts and images are served from our own domain, so loading a page does not send your information to font or image services.

## 2. Invite links

An invite link (hypefindr.net/i/your-code) shows the invite code and sends you to the Apple App Store after about two seconds. The code appears in the link and in our hosting provider's logs. We do not link a website visit to an app account. You enter the code in the app yourself. Once you reach the App Store, Apple's privacy policy applies.

## 3. Opt-out signals

The website honors Global Privacy Control. Because we do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising, the signal does not change anything today, but we will treat it as an opt-out if that ever changes.

## 4. Changes

If we add cookies, analytics or any other tracking technology to the website, we will update this notice before we do, and ask for your consent where the law requires.
