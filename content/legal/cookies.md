hypefindr.net sets no cookies for visitors and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. The one exception is our internal staff dashboard at hypefindr.net/hype-analytics: when someone who works on Hypefindr signs in to it, it sets one sign-in cookie, used only on that part of the site. This notice covers the website only. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) covers the app and the share pages at go.hyped.stream, including the random ID a share page keeps in your browser when you pull up from it.

## 2. Invite links

An invite link shared from the app starts at go.hyped.stream/i/your-code, which shows a Hypefindr card in a message preview and passes you on to hypefindr.net/i/your-code. That sends you straight to Hypefindr on the Apple App Store; hypefindr.net/get does the same without a code. The go.hyped.stream step is covered by the Privacy Policy. The code appears in the link and in our hosting provider's logs. We do not link a website visit to an app account. You enter the code in the app yourself. Once you reach the App Store, Apple's privacy policy applies.

## 3. Opt-out signals

The website honors Global Privacy Control. Because we do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising, the signal does not change anything today, but we will treat it as an opt-out if that ever changes.
