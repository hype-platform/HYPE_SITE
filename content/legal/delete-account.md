You can delete your Hypefindr account, and the data that goes with it, at any time. You can do it yourself in the app, or ask us by email if you no longer have the app.

## 1. Delete it in the app

1. Open Hypefindr and go to your profile.
2. Tap the settings gear at the top right.
3. Tap **Delete account**, then **Delete**.

It happens straight away and can't be undone. It works the same in the iPhone and Android apps.

## 2. Ask us to delete it

If you can't open the app, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the subject "Delete my account". Send it from the email address you sign in with, or include the phone number you sign in with, so we can find the account. We may ask you to confirm the request before we delete anything. We delete the account within 30 days of confirming it and reply when it's done.

## 3. What is deleted

- Your account details: name, username, date of birth, photo, bio and interests.
- Your posts, with their photo and video files.
- Plans and events you created.
- Your attending list, favorites, check-ins, milestones, points and badges.
- Your activity history, notifications, reports you filed and sign-in session records.

## 4. What is kept, and for how long

- **App usage events and error reports** are no longer linked to you, and are deleted 13 months after they were made.
- **Live chat messages** that were reported are kept for up to 90 days so the report can be reviewed. Other live chat is deleted 7 days after the live ends.
- **Feedback** you sent about the app is kept for up to 3 years without being linked to you.
- **Emails** you sent us are kept for up to 3 years.
- **Moderator decisions** about your account are kept, so we can enforce bans and answer appeals and legal requests.
- **Content reported to NCMEC** is kept for at least 1 year, as the law requires.

The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) has the full list of what we collect and how long we keep it.
